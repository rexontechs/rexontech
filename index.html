How We Work

A straightforward four-step process. You always know what stage your project is at and what comes next.

1

Understand

We start with your goals, your audience, and what the site or app actually needs to do — then agree on scope before any build begins.

2

Build

Design and development, with regular check-ins, so you see progress early and can course-correct before launch, not after.

3

Launch

Testing across devices and browsers, then deployment. We hand over a live product that you fully own.

4

Support

After go-live we remain available for updates, fixes, and improvements — your site keeps pace with your business.

About Rexontech

Rexontech is a professional web and app development company based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. We work with startups and small to medium businesses that need a dependable technical partner — not a large agency with layers of account management.

Whether you need a simple business website or a full-scale application, we focus on the same things: timely delivery, smooth performance, and support that continues after launch. You work directly with the people building your product.
